about

I'm currently a UX designer and product owner at Booz Allen Hamilton. Right now I'm working on a classified mission-support application for USEUCOM, which involves planning, operations, and crisis management for people who don't share a definition of success.

I went to Virginia Tech for computer science and graduated with minors in HCI and psychology because I wanted to understand how systems are built and how people actually use them. A couple of my projects on this site reflect that early curiosity - a maps app I designed and built, and a Spotify concept I designed with a partner.

Outside of work, I abuse my New York Times Games subscription and consume any media in the "thriller" genre - I like a puzzle or plot that makes me work to find the answer. My ideal day involves hitting the gym or pilates studio and cracking open a new book when I'm back home.
